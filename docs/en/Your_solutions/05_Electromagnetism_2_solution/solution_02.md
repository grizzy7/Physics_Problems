
### 1. Variables and Geometry
* **Current in Wire 1 ($I_1$):** $5\text{ A}$ (Upward)
* **Current in Wire 2 ($I_2$):** $5\text{ A}$ (Downward)
* **Separation ($d$):** $10\text{ cm} = 0.1\text{ m}$
* **Distance to Midpoint ($r$):** $d/2 = 5\text{ cm} = 0.05\text{ m}$
* **Permeability of Free Space ($\mu_0$):** $4\pi \times 10^{-7}\text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$

---

### 2. Determine Direction (Right-Hand Rule)
Before calculating, **we** must determine if the fields from the two wires add together or cancel out:
* **Wire 1 (Upward):** Using the right-hand rule (thumb up), the magnetic field at the midpoint (to the right of the wire) points **into the page**.
* **Wire 2 (Downward):** Using the right-hand rule (thumb down), the magnetic field at the midpoint (to the left of the wire) also points **into the page**.

Since both fields point in the same direction, **we** will add their magnitudes.



---

### 3. Step-by-Step Calculation
**Step 1: Calculate the magnitude of the field from one wire ($B_1$)**

$$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$

$$B_1 = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7})(5)}{2\pi (0.05)}$$

$$B_1 = \frac{2 \times 10^{-7} \times 5}{0.05} = \frac{10^{-6}}{0.05}$$

$$B_1 = 2 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$$

**Step 2: Sum the fields ($B_{\text{net}}$)**
Since $I_1 = I_2$ and the distance is the same, $B_1 = B_2$.
$$B_{\text{net}} = B_1 + B_2$$
$$B_{\text{net}} = 2 \times 10^{-5} + 2 \times 10^{-5}$$
$$B_{\text{net}} = 4 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$$
