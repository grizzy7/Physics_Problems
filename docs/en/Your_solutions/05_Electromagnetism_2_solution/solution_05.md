
**Given:**
- Dimensions: 10 cm × 5 cm = 0.10 m × 0.05 m

- Current: I = 2 A

- Magnetic field: B = 0.3 T

- Field is **parallel to the plane** of the loop (so θ = 90° between B and the magnetic moment vector)

**Formula:**

$$\tau = NIAB\sin\theta$$

**Area:**

$$A = 0.10 \times 0.05 = 0.005 \text{ m}^2$$

**Torque** (N = 1 turn, sin90° = 1):

$$\tau = 1 \times 2 \times 0.005 \times 0.3 \times 1$$

$$\boxed{\tau = 0.003 \text{ N·m} = 3 \times 10^{-3} \text{ N·m}}$$

**Key insight:** When B is parallel to the plane of the loop, it is **perpendicular to the magnetic moment** (which points normal to the loop), so sin θ = sin 90° = 1, giving the **maximum possible torque**.
