To find the power dissipated and the energy consumed, we use the standard electrical power and energy formulas.

### 1. Identify the Given Information

* **Resistance ($R$):** $100\ \Omega$
* 
* **Voltage ($V$):** $50\text{ V}$
* 
* **Time ($t$):** $5\text{ minutes} = 5 \times 60\text{ seconds} = 300\text{ s}$

---

### 2. Step-by-Step Calculations

**Calculate the Power ($P$):**
We use the power formula that connects voltage and resistance:


$$P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

$$P = \frac{50^2}{100} = \frac{2500}{100}$$

$$P = 25\text{ W}$$

**Calculate the Energy Consumed ($E$):**
Energy is power multiplied by time (in seconds):


$$E = P \times t$$

$$E = 25\text{ W} \times 300\text{ s}$$

$$E = 7500\text{ J} \text{ (or } 7.5\text{ kJ)}$$
