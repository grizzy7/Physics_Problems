 1. Identify the Given Information

* **Total Charge ($\Delta Q$):** $30\text{ C}$
* **Time Interval ($\Delta t$):** $2\text{ ms} = 2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ s} = 0.002\text{ s}$

---

### 2. The Formula

The average electric current ($I_{\text{avg}}$) is the total charge divided by the total time:


$$I_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}$$

---

### 3. Step-by-Step Calculation

**Step 1: Set up the fraction with standard units**


$$I_{\text{avg}} = \frac{30\text{ C}}{2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ s}}$$

**Step 2: Simplify the calculation**


$$I_{\text{avg}} = \frac{30}{0.002} = 15,000\text{ A}$$

**Step 3: Express in scientific or standard engineering notation**


$$I_{\text{avg}} = 1.5 \times 10^4\text{ A} = 15\text{ kA (kiloamperes)}$$
