
### 1. Identify the Given Information

* **Capacitor 1 ($C_1$):** $4\ \mu\text{F} = 4 \times 10^{-6}\text{ F}$
* 
* **Capacitor 2 ($C_2$):** $6\ \mu\text{F} = 6 \times 10^{-6}\text{ F}$
* 
* **Voltage ($V$):** $10\text{ V}$

---

### 2. Step-by-Step Calculation

**Step 1: Calculate the Total Equivalent Capacitance ($C_{\text{total}}$)**

For parallel circuits, **we** add the capacitances directly:


$$C_{\text{total}} = C_1 + C_2$$

$$C_{\text{total}} = 4\ \mu\text{F} + 6\ \mu\text{F} = 10\ \mu\text{F}$$

**Step 2: Calculate the Total Stored Charge ($Q_{\text{total}}$)**

Using the charge formula ($Q = C \cdot V$):


$$Q_{\text{total}} = C_{\text{total}} \cdot V$$

$$Q_{\text{total}} = (10 \times 10^{-6}\text{ F}) \times 10\text{ V}$$

$$Q_{\text{total}} = 100 \times 10^{-6}\text{ C} = 100\ \mu\text{C}$$

**Step 3: Calculate the Total Energy Stored ($W$)**

Using the potential energy formula for a capacitor ($W = \frac{1}{2} C V^2$):


$$W = \frac{1}{2} C_{\text{total}} V^2$$

$$W = \frac{1}{2} (10 \times 10^{-6}\text{ F}) \times (10\text{ V})^2$$

$$W = 0.5 \times (10 \times 10^{-6}) \times 100$$

$$W = 500 \times 10^{-6}\text{ J} = 5.0 \times 10^{-4}\text{ J} \text{ (or } 0.5\text{ mJ)}$$
