
### 1. The Breakdown

* **Speedometer Reading:** $60\text{ km/h}$
* 
* **Uncertainty Margin:** $5\%$

**Finding the Margin in km/h:**


$$60 \times 0.05 = 3\text{ km/h}$$

**Finding the Range:**

* **Low end:** $60 - 3 = 57\text{ km/h}$
* 
* **High end:** $60 + 3 = 63\text{ km/h}$

**Formal Style:**


$$\text{Speed} = 60 \pm 3\text{ km/h}$$
