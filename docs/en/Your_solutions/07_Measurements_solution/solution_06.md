
### 1. Identify the Last Digit

The thermometer reads **$25.4^\circ\text{C}$**.

* The last digit is in the **tenths place** ($0.1^\circ\text{C}$).
* Therefore, the value of the last digit (the smallest increment the device can show) is $0.1^\circ\text{C}$.

---

### 2. Step-by-Step Calculation

According to the problem, the uncertainty is half the value of this last digit:

$$\text{Absolute Uncertainty} = \frac{\text{Value of Last Digit}}{2}$$

$$\text{Absolute Uncertainty} = \frac{0.1^\circ\text{C}}{2}$$

$$\text{Absolute Uncertainty} = 0.05^\circ\text{C}$$

---

### 3. Final Measurement Representation

When writing this measurement formally, **we** express it alongside its absolute uncertainty:


$$\text{Temperature} = 25.40 \pm 0.05^\circ\text{C}$$
